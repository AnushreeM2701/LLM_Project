Easy and Medium questions are sampled evenly across the four categories
(10 per category per tier), all sourced from Hendrycks MATH. The Hard
tier uses every currently curated, category-labelled AIME question
available (51 total), which is why its category counts are uneven
(Section~\ref{sec:threats-validity}). Table~\ref{tab:hard-tier-questions}
lists every Hard-tier question by ID, category, and ground-truth final
answer; the full question text is omitted here to avoid reproducing
lengthy competition problem statements at length, and is available in
\texttt{data/processed/final\_dataset.xlsx} in the project repository
(Section~\ref{sec:pipeline}).
